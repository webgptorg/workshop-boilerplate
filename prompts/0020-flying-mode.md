Implement flying mode in the game.

When the user double-taps the spacebar, it should toggle the free flying mode.

In flying mode:
- The player can move freely in all directions, including up and down.
- Gravity is disabled.
- The player can still place and remove blocks as usual.
- The camera should follow the player's movement smoothly.
- Double-tapping the spacebar again should exit the flying mode and return to normal gameplay.
- Similar experience to Minecraft's flying mode.
